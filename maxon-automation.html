PROJECT 02 • POWER QUERY • FINANCIAL RECONCILIATION

Taking a week-long process and asking, "Does all of this really need to be manual?"

A recurring financial reconciliation involved multiple source files, historical data, formulas, financial-system information, and a very large Excel workbook. I used Power Query to turn much of the repetitive setup into a refreshable workflow.

The Original Process

Eight source files. One large workbook. A lot of updating.

The reconciliation supported financial reporting for third-party administered insurance products.

Each reporting cycle included separate files for new business and in-force business, covering four key financial and operational measures.

Premiums

Financial activity requiring reconciliation to internal reporting.

Commissions

Commission activity compared against supporting financial information.

Certificate Counts

Policy-volume information used as part of the reporting process.

Face Amounts

Coverage amounts reviewed across new and existing business.

Those four measures existed for both new and in-force business, resulting in multiple recurring source files that needed to be loaded into the appropriate areas of the workbook.

From there, formulas, historical data, internal financial information, reconciliations, and adjustments all had to work together. The full process could take roughly a week.

What I Changed

Let Power Query handle the repetitive setup.

I redesigned the file-ingestion portion of the workflow using Power Query so recurring source files could be loaded and transformed more consistently.

Instead of manually placing and updating data throughout the workbook each cycle, the workflow could refresh the required information while preserving the financial logic and review process around it.

01

Standardize the inputs

Structured recurring source files so they could feed into a repeatable data-import process.

02

Load the right data

Used Power Query to bring information into the appropriate areas of the reconciliation workbook.

03

Refresh instead of rebuild

Reduced repetitive file-loading and preparation work by using refreshable queries rather than repeating the same manual setup.

04

Keep financial review intact

Automation supported the workflow without removing reconciliation, validation, or analyst judgment from the process.

Before & After

Same financial responsibility. Less repetitive handling.

Before

  • Receive multiple recurring Excel files.
  • Manually move information into the reporting workbook.
  • Update supporting sheets and formulas.
  • Pull and incorporate additional financial data.
  • Work through reconciliation differences.
  • Repeat the setup again next reporting cycle.
→

After

  • Receive recurring source files.
  • Use Power Query to ingest and transform the data.
  • Refresh the workbook with updated information.
  • Focus analyst time on validation and reconciliation.
  • Investigate differences and prepare necessary adjustments.
  • Reuse the workflow the next reporting cycle.
Business Impact

A process measured in days instead of a week.

Time Savings

Helped reduce a reconciliation workflow that could take roughly a week to approximately one working day.

Repeatability

Created a reusable ingestion process instead of rebuilding the same file-preparation steps every reporting period.

Better Use of Analyst Time

Shifted more attention toward validation, reconciliation differences, and financial review rather than repetitive data placement.
