About Me

Finance brain. Analytics mindset.

I'm a financial analyst with experience across healthcare and insurance, supporting FP&A, budgeting, forecasting, financial reporting, internal audit, and business intelligence.

My work has ranged from managing multi-million-dollar cost centers and analyzing financial variances to improving reporting workflows with Power Query, SQL, and Python.

What I enjoy most is sitting at the intersection of finance, data, and business decisions: understanding what the numbers are saying, explaining why they changed, and figuring out what we should do next.

My general philosophy:

If I'm doing the exact same manual task every month, eventually I'm going to start wondering whether Python, Power Query, SQL, or Excel can do some of it for me.

It's not laziness. It's process improvement. 🙂
